# Product
GumShoo (Social Media Safety Detector) analyses social media content for risk.
Tools: Deepfake Analyzer (images), Sentiment Analysis (text), Fraud Detection
(scam ads and messages, bank transactions), Propaganda Detection (text).
Audience: analysts and evaluators. Tone: professional, restrained, formal.
No emojis anywhere (UI, code, comments, docs).
Every result is an estimate, never a verdict. Always show uncertainty and limitations.
